Argo CD:

We once had deployment failure in ArgoCD due to wrong image tag pushed by CI.
The pods went into ImagePullBackOff and ArgoCD showed Sync Failed.
I checked the ArgoCD diff view, found the tag mismatch, updated manifest with correct image tag and pushed to Git.
ArgoCD synced automatically, pods rolled out successfully, and I verified logs/metrics.
Later I added tag validation in Jenkins pipeline & enabled PR review to prevent similar issues.

Short + Real + Confident. Perfect for interview.

We had an issue where one microservice was unable to reach another service.
The application logs showed a DNS resolution error (getaddrinfo failure) — meaning the pod could not resolve the service name.

To troubleshoot, I went inside the pod and tested DNS resolution:

kubectl exec -it <pod-name> -n prod -- sh
nslookup customer-profile-service
curl http://customer-profile-service:8080


nslookup failed → so I checked if the service and endpoints existed:

kubectl get svc -n prod | grep customer-profile
kubectl get endpoints customer-profile-service -n prod


The service wasn’t in prod namespace, it was actually running in billing namespace.
So DNS inside Kubernetes should be:

customer-profile-service.billing.svc.cluster.local


I updated the service URL/ConfigMap with correct namespace, redeployed, and the communication worked fine.
